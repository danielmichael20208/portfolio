About Me

I am a sophomore high school student passionate about cybersecurity, robotics, and engineering. I work at Georgia Tech’s Dean of Engineering Office and compete in FTC Robotics at the Atlanta–Marist League (Top 16 teams). I’m passionate about cybersecurity, robotics, and building systems that bridge the digital and physical world. During high school I began exploring security through programming, CTFs, and hands-on hardware projects. I’m currently developing cyber tools, experimenting with embedded device security, and learning how real organizations defend against threats. My goal is to work in cybersecurity and contribute to systems that protect people, infrastructure, and technology. I believe learning should be project-driven. Everything here represents something I built, broke, tested, or experimented with. If you’re curious about any of it, feel free to reach out.
