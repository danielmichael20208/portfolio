Cyber Labs

Hands-on cybersecurity practice and ethical hacking labs. This section documents my hands-on cybersecurity training. Instead of only reading about concepts, I learn by doing: running labs, solving CTFs, analyzing traffic, and simulating attacks. These exercises helped me understand how vulnerabilities are exploited and how organizations detect and prevent threats in real environments. The labs currently focus on web exploitation, Linux privilege escalation, reconnaissance, password cracking, and forensics. Future additions will include cloud security, network intrusion detection, and embedded device security.
